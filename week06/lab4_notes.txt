During Part 4 of the practical, I encountered Error 1451 when I attempted to delete the Library and Information Science department.
 MySQL refused because a student record still referenced that department through the foreign key in the Student table. 
This restriction protects referential integrity by preventing the deletion of a parent record that is still being used by another table.
 I fixed the issue by updating the student's department to Software Engineering before deleting the Library and Information Science department.
 Another possible solution would be to use the ON DELETE CASCADE option when defining the foreign key, which would automatically delete dependent
